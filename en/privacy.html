Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 31, 2026

DingLoop values your privacy and complies with applicable laws.

1. Data We Collect

  • No account signup is required, and we do not collect direct identifiers like name or email inside the app.
  • App settings such as alarm interval and selected sound are stored locally on your device.
  • Notification permission is used to schedule local notifications.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Provide repeating alarm functionality
  • Restore your settings when reopening the app
  • Maintain app stability and quality

3. Retention and Deletion

Settings are stored on your device and are removed when the app is deleted.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except when required by law.

5. Permissions and Platform Policy

  • Notifications: Used to deliver alarms in background/lock screen.
  • Apple system behavior: Notification behavior follows iOS policy.
